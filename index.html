About us

VI Interior Designs PLC is a professional firm specializing in high-quality interior design established in February 2017 in Addis Ababa.

At VI Interior Designs PLC, we embrace a tailored approach for every project, ensuring we meet and exceed our clients' expectations. Our commitment lies in crafting unique solutions, from the smallest details to the overall design, always striving for inventive space utilization and a thoughtful selection of materials and custom finishes.

Services

Interior Design

We are dedicated to crafting interiors that prioritize health, safety, flexibility, and visual appeal across various environments, including retail spaces, offices, hotels, and residences. Our designs are crafted to foster happiness, interaction, and collaboration, enriching the experiences of all who inhabit them.

Architectural Design

Our design philosophy integrates future-focused ergonomic and financial considerations, aligning with broader strategic objectives. By prioritizing efficiency and sustainability, we aim to create high-performance buildings that offer long-term benefits to both users and owners alike.

Product Design

At VI Designs PLC, we go beyond traditional interior design by crafting custom furniture and bespoke items tailored to your unique needs and expectations. Each piece is meticulously designed to enhance the overall aesthetic and functionality of your space, ensuring a one-of-a-kind environment that is both pleasurable and visually stunning.
